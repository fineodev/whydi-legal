Privacy Policy

Whydi · Last updated: August 3, 2026
Short version: Whydi doesn't have accounts, doesn't use analytics or ad SDKs, and doesn't send your data anywhere. Everything you create — tasks, notes, annual events — stays in a local database on your device.

What data Whydi stores

Whydi stores the following directly on your device, in a local database it manages:

  • Tasks, task titles, descriptions, colors, and scheduling/recurrence settings
  • Task completion history and any excuse text you type when resolving a missed deadline
  • Annual events (birthdays, anniversaries, imported holidays) you add
  • App preferences (theme, week-start day, onboarding status)

None of this is transmitted to us, to the app developer, or to any third party. There is no account, login, or cloud sync — the data lives only on your device and in any backup you personally create using the app's own calendar export (.ics) feature.

What Whydi does not do

  • No account creation or login of any kind
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage-tracking SDKs
  • No advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers
  • No selling, sharing, or transmitting your data to third parties

Permissions Whydi requests, and why

  • Display over other apps — required for the overlay reminder to appear above whatever app you're using when a task deadline passes. Used only to draw this reminder; it does not read or record anything from other apps.
  • Post notifications — used to warn you before a deadline hits and to remind you of upcoming annual events.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) — required so deadline reminders and the overlay fire at the exact scheduled time, even if the app hasn't been opened recently.
  • Ignore battery optimizations — optional; without it, some phones' battery-saving features can prevent the overlay and reminders from firing reliably.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data — they exist solely to make the app's reminder features work correctly on your device.

Third-party links

Whydi's Settings screen includes optional links to external websites (for example, a "Buy me a coffee" support page, and this privacy policy itself). Visiting any of these links takes you outside the app to a third-party website, which has its own privacy policy and practices that Whydi does not control.

Data security and deletion

Your data is stored locally in the app's private storage area, protected by your device's own security. Uninstalling Whydi, or clearing its app data from Android's App Info screen, permanently deletes everything the app has stored.

Children's privacy

Whydi is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age — it does not collect information from anyone at all, since nothing leaves the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
