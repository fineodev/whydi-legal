Privacy Policy

Whydi · Last updated: August 9, 2026
Short version: Whydi has no accounts, no analytics, no ad SDKs, and no servers. It never sends your data anywhere, because there is nowhere to send it. Everything you create — tasks, notes, annual events — stays in a local database on your device.

What data Whydi stores

Whydi stores the following directly on your device, in a local database it manages:

  • Tasks, task titles, descriptions, colors, and scheduling/recurrence settings
  • Task completion history, including any excuse text you type when resolving a missed deadline
  • Statistics derived from that history, such as completion rates and streaks
  • Annual events (birthdays, anniversaries, imported holidays) you add
  • App preferences (theme, week-start day, onboarding status)

None of this is transmitted to us, to the app developer, or to any third party. There is no account, login, or cloud sync — the data lives only on your device, and in any backup you personally create using the app's own calendar export feature.

What Whydi does not do

  • No account creation or login of any kind
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage-tracking SDKs
  • No advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers
  • No selling, sharing, or transmitting your data to third parties
  • No network requests at all — the app functions entirely offline

Permissions Whydi requests, and why

  • Display over other apps — required for the overdue reminder to appear above whatever app you're using when a task deadline passes. Used only to draw this reminder; it does not read, record, or capture anything from other apps or from your screen.
  • Post notifications — used to warn you before a deadline hits and to remind you of upcoming annual events.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) — required so deadline reminders and the overdue screen fire at the exact scheduled time, even if the app hasn't been opened recently.
  • Ignore battery optimizations — optional; without it, some phones' battery-saving features can prevent the overdue screen and reminders from firing reliably.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data — they exist solely to make the app's reminder features work correctly on your device.

Importing and exporting your data

Whydi can export everything you've created to a standard calendar file (.ics), and can import tasks from an .ics file you choose. Both actions are started by you and happen entirely on your device:

  • When importing, Whydi reads only the single file you pick in Android's file chooser. It has no access to any other file on your device.
  • When exporting, Whydi hands the resulting file to Android's standard share sheet. Where it goes from there — saved locally, sent to another app, uploaded to a cloud drive — is entirely your choice, and anything you send it to has its own privacy policy.

Links and contacting support

Whydi's Settings screen contains links to this privacy policy and to the terms of use. Opening either takes you outside the app to a website, which has its own privacy policy and practices that Whydi does not control.

"Contact support" opens your device's own email app with a new message addressed to us. Whydi does not send anything by itself — nothing is transmitted unless you write the message and send it yourself, and we then see only what you chose to write.

Data security and deletion

Your data is stored locally in the app's private storage area, protected by your device's own security. Uninstalling Whydi, or clearing its app data from Android's App Info screen, permanently deletes everything the app has stored. Because no copy is ever sent anywhere, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no deletion request to make.

Children's privacy

Whydi is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age — it does not collect information from anyone at all, since nothing leaves the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
